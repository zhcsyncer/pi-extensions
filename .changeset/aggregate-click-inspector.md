Support clicking an entire Tools title row to expand or collapse one run in Pi 0.85+ fullscreen mode, while Ctrl+O continues to control the whole transcript. Expanded tool rows open a read-only Result/Args viewer with advanced Metadata, scrolling and structured parameter/metadata inspection. Long steers use bounded head/tail previews with a clickable omission row. Viewing never reruns a tool or changes Session messages.
